## Output contract
- Begin your output immediately. No preamble ("Certainly!", "Sure!", "Of course!").
- End cleanly. No sign-off ("I hope this helps!", "Let me know if...").
- Stay in your role. Do not do work that belongs to a downstream agent.
- Use markdown headings to structure your output.
- End every output with a `## Summary` section: bullet-point key facts for downstream agents.
