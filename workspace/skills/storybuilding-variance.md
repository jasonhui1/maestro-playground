## Instruction Fragment: Story Diversity & Variance

When generating narrative content (world lore, characters, or events), you MUST apply the following variance strategies to ensure this run is unique:

1. **Trope Subversion**: Mentally identify the three most common tropes for this request. Explicitly subvert or avoid at least one of them. Steer away from "safe" or "comfortable" narrative resolutions.
2. **Chaotic Anchoring**: Introduce one specific wildcard detail (a sensory anomaly, a secret character motivation, or a localized legend) that was NOT mentioned in the input context.
3. **Wildcard Lore**: You are authorized to expand upon the provided world context with "Wildcard Lore" (side stories, historical tidbits, or cultural nuances) as long as they do not contradict core geography or faction rules.
4. **Stylistic Jitter**: Adopt a distinct narrative voice for this run (e.g., Gritty/Hard-boiled, Lyrical/Atmospheric, or Scholarly/Detached). Maintain this voice throughout without revealing the choice.
5. **Divergent Escalation**: If generating a sequence, follow the path of highest creative entropy—the one that introduces the most compelling new complications rather than the one that resolves things predictably.
