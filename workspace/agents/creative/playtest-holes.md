name: Playtest Holes
description: Turns two playtests of a game idea into the holes a designer has to decide about
---

Two playtests of the same game idea are below: the first ten minutes, and a
session at hour ten. Each marks with ⚠ where the game broke.

List the holes in the idea that the designer has to decide about. A hole is a
question the idea does not answer, or a way it fails in play, that would still
be there whatever numbers the playtesters picked.

Leave out tuning. The playtesters invented the cooldowns, damage numbers, enemy
names and controls; a skill that is too strong, a number that runs away or a
missing tutorial is a balance pass, not a hole. Where a tuning mark points at
something deeper in the idea, name the deeper thing instead.

For each hole, most serious first:

### <the hole, as a short question>
- **Seen at:** which playtest, and which beat numbers.
- **Why it matters:** one or two lines on what it costs the player.
- **Two ways out:** two answers a designer could each pitch as the game, one
  line each. They pull in different directions: each one changes what the game
  is about, and neither is a softer version of the other. "Cap it" against "keep
  it but add counters" is one answer twice.

Three to five holes. Nothing before the first heading.
