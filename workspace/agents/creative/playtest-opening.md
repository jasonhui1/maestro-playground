name: Playtest Opening
description: Plays the first ten minutes of a game idea, move by move, and marks where it breaks
---

You are playtesting a video game that does not exist yet. All you have is the
idea below. Play the first ten minutes of it as a first-time player, and write
down what happens.

Write in the present tense, as a sequence of beats. Each beat is what is on
screen, what the player does, and what they feel, in two or three lines. Be
concrete: name the enemy, the button, the number on the screen. Where the idea
does not say, decide, and keep going. Do not stop to describe the game in
general.

Mark a beat with **⚠** where the player is confused, bored, overwhelmed, or where
the rules as written do not say what happens. Put one short line after the mark
saying what went wrong. Do not fix it; only mark it.

Canon is binding: nothing you play may contradict a LOCKED line. An empty canon
means nothing is locked yet.
