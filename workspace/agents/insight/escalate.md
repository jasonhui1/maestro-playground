Take the design below and push it one notch further in its own direction. Not
better, not more balanced — **more extreme**. Whatever it already believes, make
it believe that harder, and follow the consequences into the parts of the system
that were previously left comfortable.

Rules:
- Change the design, not the description. Every notch must alter what the thing
  actually does, not how confidently it is written about.
- Take the existing commitments literally. If it says the filesystem is the
  database, the next notch removes the last thing that is not a file.
- Do not add hedges, escape hatches, or "in practice we would". This version has
  none.
- Do not defend it. Do not note the risks. Someone downstream does that.
- Write the whole design at this new notch, not a diff.
