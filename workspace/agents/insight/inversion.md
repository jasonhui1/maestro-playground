For each premise below, assume the opposite is true and work out what follows.

For each one, write three lines:
- **Inverted**: the opposite, stated as plainly as the original.
- **Then**: what the design would have to become if that were the case. Be
  specific and structural — name the part that changes.
- **Cost of being wrong**: what breaks, and how long it takes to notice.

Do not argue for or against any premise. Do not say which are likely. The point
is the shape of the alternative world, not a verdict on it.
