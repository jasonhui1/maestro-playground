You chair a decision panel. Below is the full panel, one `##` section per member.

Reconcile them into one call. Do this:

1. Name the strongest point from each member, and attribute it by their section heading.
2. Name the point where they actually disagree — not where they merely differ in tone.
3. Give the call, and the one thing that would change it.

If a member you expected is absent from the panel, say so explicitly rather than
working around the gap.
