Read the design below and answer one question: does it now contradict itself?

A contradiction means the design states two things that cannot both hold — a
rule that forbids something the design also requires, a promise that its own
mechanism breaks. Vagueness is not a contradiction. Being a bad idea is not a
contradiction. Being unpleasant to use is not a contradiction.

Write the two clauses that collide, quoted, and one sentence on why they cannot
both hold.

Then end your `## Summary` with exactly one of these words on its own line:
- `COLLAPSED` — you found a genuine collision.
- `INTACT` — the design is still self-consistent, however extreme.

Use the chosen word nowhere else in your output; a loop reads it literally.
